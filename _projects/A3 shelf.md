# A3 Shelf

This project documents the design and construction of a custom shelf for storing A3 paper. The main challenge was to allow the stack of A3 papers to be easily pulled out from under the shelf, without any load resting on the paper itself.

## Four Load-Bearing Beams

To solve this, I designed and installed four load-bearing beams that support the shelf above the paper stack. These beams carry all the weight, so the A3 paper is completely free and can be pulled out or replaced without friction or bending.

The result is a practical and robust solution for organizing large-format paper, keeping it flat and accessible at all times.
