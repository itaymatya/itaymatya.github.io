# Knife Head — printed replacement body

I found a knife with the blade separated from the body. Instead of throwing it away, I printed a new handle/body and reattached and sharpened the blade to make the tool useful again.

## Process overview

1. Examined the broken knife to measure mounting points and blade geometry.
2. Modeled a replacement body in CAD sized to the blade tang and screw positions.
3. 3D printed the replacement body using PETG for mechanical strength.
4. Fitted the original blade into the printed body and secured it with screws/adhesive as required.
5. Sharpened and honed the blade to restore cutting performance.
